Our Technological Capabilities

We invest continuously in leading-edge dental diagnostic and surgical technologies to ensure painless, rapid, and precise treatments.

Low-Dose 3D CBCT

Captures 360-degree volumetric bone and nerve scans with 80% less radiation than traditional hospital CT equipment.

Intraoral 3D Scanners

Replaces gooey, unpleasant impression trays with painless 60-second optical scans accurate down to 10 microns.

Soft-Tissue Diode Lasers

Enables virtually bloodless, stitch-free gum recontouring with accelerated healing times and zero postoperative pain.

Hospital HEPA Filtration

Negative-pressure operatory air exchangers that replace room air every 4 minutes for absolute airborne bio-safety.
