Our Methodology

The Aura Treatment Standard

Every clinical procedure adheres to our three-tier care philosophy ensuring safety, biological preservation, and longevity.

1. Digital Diagnostics

We believe in absolute clarity. Intraoral 4K cameras and 3D scans allow you to clearly see what our clinicians see before any treatment begins.

2. Minimally Invasive

Preserving your natural tooth enamel is our priority. We employ adhesive bonding and micro-dentistry to preserve biological integrity.

3. Zero-Anxiety Comfort

From painless computer-assisted anesthesia delivery to nitrous oxide sedation, we eliminate discomfort from every procedure.
