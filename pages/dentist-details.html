Specialist Profile

Dr. Eleanor Vance, DDS, FACP

Lead Prosthodontist & Fellow of the American College of Prosthodontists specializing in high-precision cosmetic and reconstructive dentistry.

Background & Credentials

Master of Biological Esthetics

Dr. Eleanor Vance completed her Doctorate of Dental Surgery at the Harvard School of Dental Medicine, followed by a three-year specialty residency in Prosthodontics at Columbia University.

With over 14 years of clinical experience, Dr. Vance is recognized as a leader in biomimetic dentistry—an approach focused on conserving natural tooth structure through advanced adhesive protocols rather than aggressive drilling.

Key Accreditations:

  • • Fellow: American College of Prosthodontists (FACP)
  • • Active Member: American Academy of Cosmetic Dentistry (AACD)
  • • Adjunct Faculty: UCSF Division of Biomaterials
Clinical Focus

Areas of Specialization

Dr. Vance's clinical focus spans conservative smile makeovers, complex bite rehabilitation, and ceramic bonding.

Porcelain Veneers

Hand-crafted ceramic laminates designed under dental operating microscopes for seamless natural translucency and longevity.

Digital Smile Design

Predictable aesthetic simulations that let patients test and approve their aesthetic changes before physical preparation.

Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

Comprehensive neuromuscular and occlusal reconstruction for patients with severe tooth wear, trauma, or bite collapse.
