Specialty Procedure

Cosmetic Dentistry & Porcelain Veneers

Bespoke aesthetic smile transformations combining master ceramic artistry, 3D computer simulation, and conservative enamel preparation.

Procedure Overview

Artistry Meets Dental Engineering

Cosmetic dentistry at Aura Dental Studio is more than surface aesthetics—it is a harmonious balance of biological health, jaw kinematics, and facial symmetry.

Using our Digital Smile Design (DSD) protocol, we capture high-definition 3D scans of your teeth and facial contours. Before any clinical tooth modification occurs, you preview a realistic 3D mockup of your future smile directly on screen.

Key Treatment Phases:

  • • Session 1: Digital 3D scans, facial photographic mapping & aesthetic simulation.
  • • Session 2: Trial smile evaluation & micro-conservative enamel preparation.
  • • Session 3: Permanent resin-matrix bonding of hand-layered ceramic veneers.

Frequently Asked

Is the veneer procedure painful?

No. The treatment is performed under gentle local anesthesia or mild sedation. Most patients experience zero discomfort during or after the visit.

How many visits are required?

Most smile transformations are completed across 2 to 3 appointments scheduled over a 2-week span.

Are financing options available?

Yes. We offer 0% interest dental financing plans and accept major dental insurance benefits.
